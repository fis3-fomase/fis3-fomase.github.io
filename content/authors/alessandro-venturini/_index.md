Alessandro Venturini is a PhD student fascinated by the craft of building
software. His main interests are software engineering and functional
programming, particularly the ways in which programming languages and good
design principles can help us build better software.

He enjoys going beyond simply using tools and technologies: he likes
understanding the ideas behind them, experimenting with them, and sometimes
building his own. He is especially interested in developing tools for developers
and in the possibility of making software development itself more reliable,
expressive, and enjoyable.
